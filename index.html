About Me

Forest scientist
rooted in the field

Forest-science professional from Bangladesh with deep expertise in ecology, forestry, and biodiversity. Currently working as a Research Consultant at the Center for Environmental and Geographic Information Services (CEGIS) under the Ministry of Water Resources.

My work spans forest health monitoring, biodiversity surveys, afforestation/reforestation initiatives, landscape restoration planning, environmental impact assessments, and ecosystem services evaluation — across the Sundarbans, Chittagong Hill Tracts, wetlands, and coastal regions.
